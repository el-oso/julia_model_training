**Key design decisions:**

- **`get!(counts, code, 0)`** — single lookup to read+increment (avoids double-hash of `get` + `set`)
- **`UInt8(seq[i])`** — avoids `Char`→`String` conversion overhead in the hot loop
- **Pre-compiled `_BASE_TABLE`** — constant Dict lookup for base→bit mapping
- **Bitmask `(UInt64(1) << (2k)) - 1`** — keeps only the lowest 2k bits as new bases shift in
- **Decode extracts from highest to lowest bit** (first base is in the lowest 2 bits due to left-shift accumulation)
