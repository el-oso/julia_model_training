**Grammar (Python precedence, `^` tighter than unary minus):**

```
expr   = term (('+' | '-') term)*
term   = factor (('*' | '/') factor)*
factor = ('-' | '+') factor | power
power  = atom ('^' unary_expr)*   -- right-associative
unary_expr = ('-' | '+') unary_expr | power
atom   = '(' expr ')' | NUMBER
```

**Key design decisions:**
- `power` calls `unary_expr` on `^`'s right operand; `unary_expr` calls `power` (→ `atom`, no `*`/`/`) — this mutual recursion is what makes `^` bind tighter than `*` and unary minus
- `factor` handles unary at the bottom level (Python's approach), giving `-2^2 = -(2^2) = -4`
- `Ref{Int}` for position avoids struct mutability issues and closure overhead
- Inline lexing (no token list) keeps it fast: **99,999 chars in ~7 ms** (warm), **~156 ms** (cold)

**Malformed input** throws `ArgumentError` with a description and the byte position, e.g. `"unexpected character '×' at position 42"`.
