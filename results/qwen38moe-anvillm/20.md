Done. Here's `bf.jl` — a fast Brainfuck interpreter in idiomatic Julia:

**Architecture:**
- **3-pass compiler** → compact integer-encoded IR (10 opcodes)
  1. Match brackets via stack
  2. Simulate emit (folding + `[-]` opt) to map source positions → IR positions
  3. Emit the final IR
- **Interpreter** — tight `while` loop over the IR with `UInt8` tape (30000 cells, wraparound via `mod(..., 256)`)

**Optimisations:**
- Folds consecutive `+`/`-`/`<`/`>` into single instructions (e.g. 255 `+` → one `INCR 255`)
- `[-]` → single `CLEAR` instruction (verified `code[i-count-1] == '['` to avoid false matches)
- Bracket jumps precomputed to IR positions (±2 for the operand slot)
