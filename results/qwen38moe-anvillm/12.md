This is expected — `LinearAlgebra.lu` delegates to LAPACK's `dgetrf` (BLAS-optimized native code), while our pure-Julia version does row-swap loops and scalar operations without SIMD/vectorization. The algorithm is correct and idiomatic; closing the gap would require BLAS-level intrinsics or `@inbounds`/`@simd` annotations.

**Key design decisions:**
- **Partial pivoting** with `max_abs` initialized to `abs(A[k,k])` (avoids the k=m edge case where the inner loop is empty)
- **In-place** on A with L stored in the lower triangle and U in the upper triangle, then extracted
- **`lu_solve`** permutes `b` with `p`, forward-substitutes `L*y = pb`, then backward-substitutes `U*x = y`
- **Generic `T`** via `zero(T)` / `one(T)` — works for `Float64`, `Rational{BigInt}`, etc.
